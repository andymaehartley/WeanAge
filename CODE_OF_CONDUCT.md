# Code of conduct

WeanAge is used and built by people from many backgrounds, disciplines and career stages.
Everyone taking part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or at events) is expected to:

* be respectful and patient, and assume good intent;
* give and take criticism of ideas, code and methods constructively, never personally;
* credit other people's work, data and ideas;
* make room for newcomers and for people whose first language is not English.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and unwelcome sexual attention are not tolerated.

If something goes wrong, contact the maintainer (Annabelle Hartley) through GitHub. Reports are
handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or block people who
break this code.

This code is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant (contributor-covenant.org).
